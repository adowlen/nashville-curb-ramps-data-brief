## Held copy - lead with the killer number

Only 9,103 of Nashville's 18,388 audited curb-ramp records are recorded as conforming. 4,035 are recorded No - and 961 records describe a location with no ramp at all.

District 18 records the lowest conforming share at 36.3% (273 of 752). District 11 records a 39.8% conforming share and the highest no-ramp share: 134 of its 704 records (19.0%). The most common recorded reason on a No record is a landing over 2% (544 records under that exact explanation). Audit-era shares are printed with their warning in the brief: an audit date is not a construction date, and the 2023 wave alone is 43.0% of the layer.

Full brief, data, and audit: https://github.com/adowlen/nashville-curb-ramps-data-brief
