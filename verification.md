## Pre-flight audit (Standards Section 10, hard gate)

Audit script: code/audit_section10.py; full output: data/audit_output.txt. Ran BEFORE stats and prose. Result: PASS on every applicable check.

- Zero/null sentinel handling: this layer's numeric fields are measurements, not codes. Slope 0 is a recorded measurement (a flat slope) and is kept; missing slope values are excluded from each control's base, and each control prints its base. DetectableWarning is recorded 0 on 9,702 records, 1 on 8,056, and blank on 630; blank is mapped to unknown and excluded from every DetectableWarning share (coded base 17,758, printed wherever the figure appears). DateAudited carries no sentinel dates (no year before 1990, no raw 0 or negative values among the 17,664 recorded dates); 724 records carry no audit date and form their own era group rather than entering any era denominator silently.
- Polarity / matched-vs-excluded for the conformance filter: matched sample (first 3 records with normalized ConformID = Yes) and excluded sample (first 3 with any other normalized category) are printed in data/audit_output.txt; the audit asserts every matched record normalizes to Yes.
- Numerator/denominator unit agreement: every share divides ramp records by ramp records on a printed base - all 18,388 records, the 16,789 determined-category records, the 17,524 district-known records, an audit-era base, or the 17,758 DetectableWarning-coded records. No length, area, population, or corner denominator is used anywhere; the layer carries none.
- Counter of ALL unique values for every categorical column used: ConformID (13 raw values), ProjectType (10), Material (12), StandardID (23), FlushMountID (19), Side (19), ServiceDistrict (5), Zone (6), CouncilDistrict (37), DetectableWarning (3), and the Has* slope flags - full counters in data/audit_output.txt. The counters, not assumptions, drove the normalization: ConformID merges only case/spelling variants of the same recorded category (including the misspelled "Technically Infeasable", 221 records); Unknown (470) and Blank (168) stay on the full denominator, and No Ramp is never merged into No.
- Physical scale (Section 10.2): the layer is a record inventory, not a length or area inventory; the physical scale is the record count itself (18,388 audited records, 17,524 of them district-known across 35 districts). No centerline-mile or unit total exists in this layer and none is claimed.
- Join hygiene: NO external join was performed. The sidewalk comparison uses published figures from the 2026-11-10 sidewalks brief only, district by district on each layer's own printed base, because both layers use Metro Council districts; no combined rate is computed (different populations and units). No district-level crash comparison is attempted (crash geography is precinct/ZIP; no verified crosswalk exists in this package).
- Internal control groups (Section 10.2): (1) District totals: the CouncilDistrict counter sums to 18,388 = the pulled row count (asserted in the audit). (2) Landing-slope control: among records recorded No with a landing measurement, the maximum recorded landing slope exceeds 2 percent on 42.7 percent (1,705 of 3,993), against 6.9 percent (629 of 9,077) among records recorded Yes. (3) Perpendicular-slope control: the larger recorded perpendicular slope exceeds 8.3 percent on 28.6 percent (1,090 of 3,808) of No records with a perpendicular measurement, against 3.3 percent (291 of 8,706) of Yes records. ## Methods checklist

- Base rates and exposure: conformance is reported on two printed bases (all 18,388 records; the 16,789 determined-category records), district shares on the district-known base, and DetectableWarning shares on the coded base. The layer holds only audited locations: it contains no count of unaudited corners and no walking-volume measure, so no completeness rate and no crash rate is computed. Stated in the brief Methods and in the district and comparison sections.
- Direction of causality: no causal claim is made. In particular, no claim connects audit era to construction quality (DateAudited is not a construction date, and no construction-date field exists), and no claim connects ramp conformance to crashes.
- Category and definition changes over the window: the layer accumulates audit waves from 1998 to 2025, so recording practice can vary by wave; the brief documents the variation it can measure instead of computing a trend - 13 raw ConformID values with case/spelling variants, a single 2023 wave holding 43.0 percent of all records and concentrated in Districts 19, 21, 17, and 6 (with 853 of its records district-blank), the No Ramp category appearing only in the 2020-2026 era group (343 dated records) and the undated group (618 records), and Unknown conformance concentrated in the 2020-2026 group (389 of 470).
- Exact date range: inventory snapshot pulled November 11, 2026; service data last edited November 10, 2026. DateAudited range July 9, 1998 to May 6, 2025 (America/Chicago local dates); DateReAudited range July 4, 2022 to May 27, 2024 on 1,338 records. Both printed in the brief.
- Match rates: no join, so no join match rate applies. Field completeness printed instead: conformance determined 91.3 percent (16,789 of 18,388, counting the four determined categories; No Ramp, Unknown, and Blank make up the rest), district known 95.3 percent (17,524 of 18,388), audit date recorded 96.1 percent, DetectableWarning coded 96.6 percent.
- Small counts: district figures are printed for all 35 districts in data/district_panel.csv; the brief's district claims rest on districts with 123 or more records (smallest base among the districts named in prose is District 34 at 123, which is named only in the chart's 100+ filter, not in prose claims - prose names Districts 18, 11, 21, 5, 19, and 35, with bases 752, 704, 1,376, 904, 2,212, and 539). No group comparison in the brief rests on fewer than 30 cases; the smallest comparison base printed in prose is the 343 dated No Ramp records, printed with its count.
- Official definitions: the layer metadata defines no ConformID category, no DetectableWarning coding, and no ProjectType code list. The brief assigns no definitions the metadata does not carry and asks for them in Who can act. The 2 percent and 8.3 percent thresholds used in the controls are taken from the layer's own ConformExplain strings, not imported from an external standard.
